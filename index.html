About Me

I am a scientific instrumentation professional with hands-on experience in the installation, troubleshooting, maintenance, and technical support of isotope ratio mass spectrometry systems, inlet systems, elemental analyzers, and associated laboratory instrumentation. In my current role supporting research instrumentation within the Department of Earth, Environmental & Planetary Sciences (DEEPS) at Brown University, I focus on system reliability, analytical performance, and practical scientific problem-solving. My interests extend beyond maintaining systems to understanding, improving, and thinking ahead about the future of scientific instrumentation.

Scientific Instrumentation Development Initiative

Connecting People. Developing Instruments. Advancing Science.

My professional interests extend beyond the operation, maintenance and troubleshooting of scientific analytical instruments. I am particularly interested in the engineering and development of new analytical systems, custom IRMS inlets, automated sample preparation systems and innovative laboratory processes.

Through my work as an Isotope Mass Spectrometer Specialist at Brown University, and my previous experience in scientific instrumentation and engineering, I have developed a strong interest in bringing engineering concepts into practical laboratory applications.

My Vision

I am exploring the establishment of the Scientific Instrumentation Development Network (SIDN) — an independent collaborative community initially focused on stable isotope mass spectrometry and related analytical instrumentation.

The objective is to connect technically motivated professionals, including research engineers, laboratory technicians, instrumentation specialists, laboratory managers and scientists, who share an interest in developing and improving scientific analytical systems.

Areas of Interest

  • Custom analytical inlet development for IRMS.
  • Automated sample preparation and analytical workflows.
  • Vacuum systems, gas handling and instrument integration.
  • Electronics, embedded controls and laboratory automation.
  • Instrument troubleshooting, modification and performance improvement.
  • Development of new analytical methods and experimental configurations.

Collaboration Approach

I am interested in both contributing to existing instrumentation-development projects and initiating new collaborative developments with like-minded professionals.

The initial focus is on connecting stable isotope instrumentation professionals, with the longer-term vision of expanding into other scientific instrumentation disciplines and international research communities.

Looking to Connect

Looking to connect with professionals who enjoy designing, building, modifying and advancing scientific instruments.
